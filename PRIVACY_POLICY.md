## 1. Information Collection and Use

Our App is designed to provide a collection of utility tools (such as QR/Barcode scanning, calculations, local storage management, and security authentication). We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our App to you.

### Types of Data Collected:

#### A. Camera & Scanner Data (`CAMERA`, `mobile_scanner`)
- **Usage:** The App requests access to your device's camera solely to enable QR code scanning, barcode scanning, and related utility features.
- **Data Handling:** Camera feeds and scanned images/codes are processed locally in real-time on your device. **We do not store, upload, or share any camera images, videos, or scanned personal data** on external servers.

#### B. Biometric & Local Authentication Data (`local_auth`, `flutter_secure_storage`)
- **Usage:** The App may offer features secured by biometric authentication (such as fingerprint or face unlock) or local device passcode.
- **Data Handling:** Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system (Android BiometricPrompt API). We do not collect, store, or transmit your biometric data (fingerprints, face data) or credentials. Secure tokens or preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage (`flutter_secure_storage`).

#### C. Local Storage & Files (`shared_preferences`, `hive`, `file_picker`, `path_provider`)
- **Usage:** The App stores app preferences, user settings, and temporary utility data locally on your device.
- **Data Handling:** All data saved through local storage remains strictly on your device. You have full control over this data, and it is deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the App.

#### D. Internet Access (`INTERNET`)
- Used strictly for core utility features that require network connectivity (if applicable). No personal data or user tracking information is transmitted to external advertising networks.
