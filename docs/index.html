1. Information Collection and Use

Our App is designed to provide a collection of utility tools (such as QR/Barcode scanning, calculations, local storage management, and security authentication). We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our App to you.

Types of Data Collected:

  • Camera & Scanner Data (CAMERA, mobile_scanner): The App requests access to your device's camera solely to enable QR code scanning, barcode scanning, and related utility features. Camera feeds and scanned images/codes are processed locally in real-time on your device. We do not store, upload, or share any camera images, videos, or scanned personal data on external servers.
  • Biometric & Local Authentication Data (local_auth, flutter_secure_storage): The App may offer features secured by biometric authentication (such as fingerprint or face unlock) or local device passcode. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system (Android BiometricPrompt API). We do not collect, store, or transmit your biometric data or credentials.
  • Local Storage & Files (shared_preferences, hive, file_picker): The App stores app preferences, user settings, and temporary utility data locally on your device. All data saved through local storage remains strictly on your device.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used strictly for core utility features that require network connectivity. No personal data or user tracking information is transmitted to external advertising networks.
